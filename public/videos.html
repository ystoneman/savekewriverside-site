Kew Riverside Parent Voices

Share a video

Help families choosing a school see everyday life at Kew Riverside. No script: 30–90 seconds on your phone is plenty.

Share a video in 2 steps

For families choosing a school. May be posted on YouTube after Yann checks it. Adults recording themselves only. No Google or Dropbox sign-in required.

  1. Step 1: Give permission. Your email and a few ticks.

    Give permission ↗︎

  2. Step 2: Upload your video. After you press Submit, the form gives you the Dropbox upload link.

Views on the closure proposal? A video is not an official council response, so put them in yours: Respond to the council by 16 October 2026 ↗︎

Prefer to speak privately? Contact Yann privately without submitting a video.

Already saved your permissions? Upload your video. Use the same email in both steps. One video per permission form. Your saved permissions still apply, including any earlier private-only choice, even if you upload later.

New submissions require your explicit YouTube permission. News-media and paid-ads permissions are optional. Nothing is published automatically. Aim for under 100 MB.

A place to start

Pick one thought.
Make it yours.

  1. “We chose Kew Riverside because…”
  2. “A normal day for my child here looks like…”
  3. “What has mixed-age learning been like for your child?”
  4. “What I’d tell a parent visiting for the first time…”

What happens after you send it?

  1. 01

    It arrives privately.

    Your video goes to Yann’s private Dropbox; your permissions stay in Google Forms. Other contributors cannot see them. Yann matches both using your email, upload details and any filename you provide.

  2. 02

    Yann watches it.

    He checks the matching permissions, reviews the video personally and may contact you about edits. An unmatched upload stays private. Nothing is published automatically.

  3. 03

    You choose what can be shared.

    For new submissions, your required YouTube permission lets Yann post it on Kew Riverside Parent Voices on YouTube. Publication is not guaranteed. He doesn’t post videos mainly about the proposal and will suggest another route for them. Separate optional choices let Yann supply your video and chosen credit to news organisations covering Kew Riverside’s future, or use it in paid ads on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. You can still send your video without either.

A few simple recording tips

Find a quiet spot, face a window and hold your phone steady at eye level. Portrait or landscape is fine. Speak naturally; no editing, music or special equipment needed.

Record only yourself. No children or other people who can be identified should be seen in the picture or heard in the background. Leave out children’s names, health or SEND details, private records and allegations about people who can be identified. Check the background for addresses, documents and photographs.

Use your own recording and material you have permission to share. Speak from your own experience; threats, abuse, impersonation and spam are not welcome.

Your name, permission and removal

Enter the same email address in the permission form and Dropbox. The filename question is optional if your phone does not show it. Your email, filename and the name entered at upload stay private. The form has a separate, optional public credit: a first name or nickname is fine, or leave it blank. Your face and voice can still identify you.

New submissions require YouTube publication permission. Videos can share parent experiences, raise awareness and help prospective families learn about the school. You let Yann trim pauses and add accurate captions without changing your meaning. Other substantive edits need your agreement. Earlier saved permissions keep their original scope.

News-media permission is optional and unchecked. If you select it, Yann may supply your video and chosen public credit to news organisations reporting on Kew Riverside’s future. They may use the recording and excerpts in broadcast and online coverage, with accurate captions and edits that keep your meaning. Your email and other private contact details are not shared. News organisations control their own publications and may need their own release or privacy arrangements.

Paid-ads permission is optional and unchecked. If you select it, Yann may use your video, or captioned excerpts that keep your meaning, with your chosen credit, in paid ads on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. The ads invite families to learn about or visit Kew Riverside and encourage people to take part in decisions about its future; they are not fundraising appeals. He shows you the exact excerpt and ad wording before an ad first runs, and uses it only if you agree.

Ads reach local people who did not choose to watch and show which account placed them; some platforms also show who paid. They may attract public comments, which Yann moderates. Platforms may keep ads in public ad libraries after they end. Yann runs and pays for them personally; he is a PTA member, but the ads are not run for the school or PTA. Ads stop by 30 September 2028 at the latest. Earlier saved permissions do not include paid ads; to add it for an earlier video, contact Yann privately. Read the paid-ads details.

You can withdraw permission, change your credit or ask for removal. You can withdraw YouTube, news-media or paid-ads permission separately. Yann can remove his YouTube upload, stop further media sharing, pause or stop ads using your video (normally within two working days) and contact known recipients about your request. He cannot guarantee recall of broadcasts, ad-library records or copies made by others. Read the full notice.

Trouble uploading, or using an older link?

No account is needed for the two-step route above. The permission confirmation is not an upload receipt: follow its Dropbox link, choose your video and wait for Dropbox to confirm the upload. Use the same email in both steps. Keep a 30–90 second recording under 100 MB where possible; try a shorter clip or a reliable connection if the upload fails.

The original Google video form is still open if you already have that link or prefer it. It requires Google sign-in and accepts one video up to 1 GB, with one response per account. New responses use the same required YouTube and optional news-media and paid-ads choices. Earlier recorded choices remain valid. A complete submission there already includes its own permissions; you do not need to submit it again through Dropbox.

If either service is full or unavailable, or you want to replace a video, contact Yann privately to arrange an alternative. Please do not put a private video link in the public ideas board.

You can also write a community letter without an account.
